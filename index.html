Gardening · Hydroponics · Slow living

🌱 About this app

Kebun Hidroponik Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner, Antonius Junaidi (eMobile Indonesia), to post short videos he creates about home gardening, hydroponics (pakcoy, melon and other vegetables) and slow living to his own TikTok account.

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic): signs in the owner's TikTok account.
  • Content Posting API (video.upload, video.publish): uploads and publishes the owner's finished videos.
  • Nothing is posted until the owner explicitly approves each post.

🔑 Step 1: Connect your account

Sign in with your TikTok account to allow this app to post your gardening videos.

You will be redirected to TikTok to review and approve the requested permissions.
