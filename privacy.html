1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how eMobile Indonesia, operated by Antonius Junaidi, Indonesia (“we”, “us”) handles information in its TikTok content-publishing application (the “Service”).

2. Information we collect

  • TikTok basic profile information (such as open ID, display name and avatar) provided through TikTok Login Kit, to identify the connected account.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, to upload and publish on the connected account.
  • Videos and captions that you choose to publish.

We do not collect passwords, contacts, location, payment details, or any other TikTok data.

3. How we use it

Only to authenticate with TikTok and to upload and publish the videos and captions you select (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information for advertising. There is no ads tracking or profiling. Data is processed only by TikTok and by the service providers needed to run the Service (for example our integration provider Composio, which stores tokens securely), and only to provide the Service or when required by law.

5. Storage and retention

Tokens are stored securely and kept only while the account is connected. Video files are kept only as long as needed to complete the upload. When you revoke access, the tokens stop working and are deleted.

6. Your choices and rights

You can revoke access anytime in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). You may also ask us to access or delete your data by emailing us. We handle personal data in line with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP No. 27/2022).

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), to protect your information.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
